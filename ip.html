Merchandise

(a) The Artist grants the Producer a non-exclusive licence for 12 years commencing on the first commercial release of the Master(s) on a worldwide basis, to use the Artist's name, approved likeness, logos and approved artwork (Artist Materials) on merchandise (Merchandise) made and sold by or for the Producer.

(b) The Producer must get the Artist's written approval of each Merchandise design and product before it is made or offered for sale. The Artist must not unreasonably withhold or delay approval.

(c) The Producer will pay the Artist 25% of the Net Profit from all sales of Merchandise. Net Profit means all amounts the Producer actually receives from sales of Merchandise, less: GST; refunds and returns; and the reasonable direct costs the Producer actually pays to third parties to design, make, ship and sell the Merchandise (including platform and payment processing fees), but the Producer may not deduct its own overheads, staff costs or any costs of the music production services under this agreement.

(d) Within 60 days after the end of 30 June and 31 December, the Producer will give the Artist a statement showing sales, deductions and Net Profit, and pay the Artist's share after receiving the Artist's tax invoice. The Producer must keep accurate records, and the Artist may inspect them once in any 12-month period on reasonable notice.

(e) The Artist keeps all rights in the Artist Materials. When this agreement ends, the licence ends, except that the Producer may sell existing stock, and the Artist's share of Net Profit is still payable on those sales.
